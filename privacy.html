Privacy

Mise Ledger is operated by Kyvex Technology LLC, Milwaukee, Wisconsin. We process course data on behalf of the institution that buys the licence — the institution is the controller, we are the processor.

Last updated 17 November 2026. A lawyer and the campus privacy office review the final text before the first contract.

What we store

CategoryWhat it is
Staff accountsName, email, role, hashed password, last sign-in.
StudentsName, email, section, course. No date of birth, no government identifiers, no payment data.
Course workNotebook answers, lab submissions, references, photos a student uploads, attendance, exam attempts, scores.
Kitchen operationsInventory, deliveries, temperatures, purchase orders, receipts, waste logs.

That is the whole list. We do not run advertising trackers and we do not build profiles of students.

Who can see it

  • People inside that institution, by role, enforced on the server — not in the browser.
  • One institution can never reach another institution's data. This is covered by automated tests that run on every change.
  • Our own staff do not read course content except to fix a fault a customer reported, and only with that customer's permission.

What we never do

  • No sale of data.
  • No marketing use of student records.
  • No training of AI models on student work. The only AI features run on instructor material (their notes and their course text) and on public nutrition data from the USDA.
  • No third-party analytics that profile individuals.

Retention and deletion

Data belongs to the institution for as long as the agreement lasts. On request we return a full export (JSON and CSV) and delete everything within 30 days, with written confirmation.

Your rights

Students and staff ask their institution first — it holds the record. We answer the institution's request within five business days.
