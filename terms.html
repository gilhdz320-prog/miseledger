These terms cover a licence of the Mise Ledger software by an institution. The licence is signed by the institution, not by individual students.

1. What you get

A non-exclusive, non-transferable right for one institution to use Mise Ledger for its own teaching kitchens, for the term and number of courses on the order form.

2. What we do not promise

Mise Ledger records what happened in a kitchen. It is not a food-safety certification, not a substitute for an inspection, and not legal or medical advice. The institution remains responsible for its own compliance with health rules and with FERPA.

3. Availability

We aim for 99.5% monthly availability, excluding scheduled maintenance announced in advance. A pilot runs on the same infrastructure and the same target.

4. Data

The institution owns its data. We process it as described in the Privacy notice and, where education records are involved, under a signed data processing agreement.

5. Acceptable use

6. Fees and renewal

Fees are stated on the order form and invoiced annually in advance. A pilot is free. Renewal is annual unless either side gives 30 days' notice.
