Questions people ask

Before the first sign-in

Where does the data live?

In a US region, on managed infrastructure. One institution cannot see another — the separation is enforced on the server and tested on every change.

Is student data protected?

Notebooks, grades and attendance are education records handled under FERPA, with export and deletion on request. No advertising, no AI training on student work.

How long does onboarding take?

One hour with the instructor: the roster goes in, the first week is planned, and the QR for supplies is printed. The TA tour is ready the same day.

What if it does not work for us?

You keep the export of your data and you stop. No lock-in, no long contract — the pilot is one semester.
