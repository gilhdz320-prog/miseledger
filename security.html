Security

What we do to protect institutional data, written for the person who has to sign off on it.

Last updated 17 November 2026. This page describes the system as built, not a certification. We do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001.

Isolation between institutions

Every row carries the institution it belongs to, and every request is filtered by the institution of the signed-in account on the server. A student of one institution cannot read another's data even with a hand-crafted request; the automated test suite includes the attempt.

Accounts and access

  • Staff sign in with email and password; passwords are hashed with scrypt, never stored in clear.
  • Students do not get passwords: staff issue a one-time code and PIN, which is redeemed once and scoped to that student.
  • Roles (owner, instructor, TA, comptroller, student) are enforced on the server, never only in the interface.

Transport and storage

  • HTTPS everywhere; HSTS on the application domain.
  • Encryption at rest at the database provider (Supabase, US region).
  • Uploaded files are served from a private bucket, not a public path.

Backups

The database provider takes daily point-in-time backups. A restore is tested before each semester.

Change control

Every change runs against 39 automated test suites and 154 browser checks before it reaches production. The audit log of the application records who changed what, and can be exported.

Incident response

  • We notify the institution within 48 hours of learning of an incident affecting its data.
  • The notice says what was affected, what we did, and what the institution should do.
  • We keep a written record of the incident and the fix.

What we do not have

No SOC 2 report, no ISO 27001 certificate, no single sign-on yet. If your procurement requires any of these, say so early and we will tell you honestly whether we can meet it — rather than discover it during a review.
