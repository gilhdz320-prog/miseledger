Security

What we do to protect institutional data, written for the person who has to sign off on it.

Last updated 17 November 2026. This page describes the system as built, not a certification. We do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001.

Isolation between institutions

Every row carries the institution it belongs to, and every request is filtered by the institution of the signed-in account on the server. A student of one institution cannot read another's data even with a hand-crafted request; the automated test suite includes the attempt.

Accounts and access

  • Staff sign in with email and password; passwords are hashed with scrypt, never stored in clear.
  • Students do not get passwords: staff issue a one-time code and PIN, which is redeemed once and scoped to that student.
  • Roles (owner, instructor, TA, comptroller, student) are enforced on the server, never only in the interface.
  • Two-step verification (authenticator app, RFC 6238) is required for owners, instructors and comptrollers, with one-time recovery codes.
  • After 10 failed sign-ins in 15 minutes the account is locked for 15 minutes; wrong two-step codes and student codes are limited the same way.
  • Sessions live on the server and end after 30 minutes of inactivity. Signing out, a password reset or a role change ends every session of that user.

Transport and storage

  • HTTPS everywhere; HSTS with preload on both domains.
  • Content Security Policy and related security headers; every script and font is served from our own domain, with no third-party CDN.
  • Encryption at rest at the database provider (Supabase, US region).
  • Uploaded files are served from a private bucket, not a public path.

Backups

The database provider takes daily point-in-time backups. A restore is tested before each semester.

Change control

Every change runs against 40 automated test suites, 155 browser checks and GitHub CodeQL static analysis; an OWASP ZAP baseline scan runs on every release and weekly, and Dependabot watches dependencies. The audit log records who changed what, from which IP address, including sign-in, failed attempts, lockouts and every use of an AI feature (without copying its content). The database itself rejects any edit or deletion of a log entry, and the log can be exported.

Incident response

  • We notify the institution within 48 hours of learning of an incident affecting its data.
  • The notice says what was affected, what we did, and what the institution should do.
  • We keep a written record of the incident and the fix.

What we do not have

No SOC 2 report, no ISO 27001 certificate, and no single sign-on yet (it is on the roadmap in the Trust Center). Two-step verification is in place and required for staff. If your procurement requires any of these, say so early and we will tell you honestly whether we can meet it — rather than discover it during a review.
